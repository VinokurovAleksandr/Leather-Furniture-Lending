About Us

★★★★★ 4.8 (894 Ratings)

Restoration is more than repair - it's an investment in what lasts. Professional restoration not only saves thousands compared to replacement but also preserves the originality, texture, and history of your piece.

We are specialists

We are specialists in leather restoration, serving clients who value craftsmanship, precision, and authenticity. Using premium Italian materials and advanced restoration techniques, we bring life back to furniture, handbags, and collectible leather pieces. Each item is fully insured from pickup to delivery, ensuring total peace of mind.

We use exclusive techniques

Our exclusive Spa Service renews handbags, wallets, and small leather goods through deep cleaning, conditioning, gentle hardware polishing, and pigment touch-ups that refresh color and conceal wear.

We are collaborative

We proudly work with museums, collectors, and private clients who trust us to preserve the integrity and beauty of their valuable items. From fine pigment restoration to hardware re-plating in gold, chrome, or silver, our work combines artistry and technical mastery.
